Transformative and Social Design?

I am a problem seeker by heart and a designer by education. Merging these two perspectives has led to a practice centered on understanding human behavior and societal structures. I do not accept systems rooted in legacy thinking or limited knowledge. I strongly believe design is a great tool to spread wisdom and open up possibilities that can change the way we think and therefore act. So welcome to my humble abode where design serves as both a tool of constructive resistance and a driver of systematic change!

I am Moe Sand, a non-binary designer located in Sweden, aiming towards an intersection of pedagogy, design and social change/engagement.

I use design as a tool of constructive resistance and systematic change. My focus is on creating tools/objects/concepts that make complex topics or issues more accessible.
