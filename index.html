Transformative and Social Design?

I am a problemseeker by heart and a designer by education. Merging these two perspectives has led to a practice centered on understanding human behavior and societal structures. I do not accept systems rooted in legacy thinking or limited knowledge. I strongly believe design is a great tool to spread wisdom and open up possibilities that can change the way we think and therefore act. So welcome to my humble abode where design serves as both a tool of constructive resistance and a driver of systematic change!

About Moe Sand

Designer & Problemseeker

My work spans across transformative design, social structures, and critical practice. I focus on questioning existing systems and creating space for alternative futures through design intervention.

Core Focus Areas

  • Systemic Research: Uncovering hidden patterns and legacy structures.
  • Constructive Resistance: Using design as a medium for critical discourse.
  • Social Transformation: Designing methods that empower collective action.

Based in Sweden — Available for research, collaborations, and consulting.
